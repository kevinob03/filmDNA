## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-modern cinematic neon visual language rooted in darkroom observation, optical physics, and luminous projection craft. It merges dark glassmorphism with high-contrast neon spectral elements, creating an immersive, technical, and nocturnal environment tailored for cinephiles, digital auteurs, and analytical creative tools.

The interface evokes the sensation of sitting in an ultra-calibrated digital color-grading suite or laser projection deck. Pitch-black voids and deep charcoal glass layers frame electric neon light signals that cut through the dark with surgical clarity. The personality is confident, high-fidelity, visionary, and exactingly engineered.

## Colors

The color system is engineered around darkroom optical physics, using pitch-black voids punctuated by laser-sharp chromatic neon wavelengths.

### Spectral Lead & Accents
- **Primary Accent — Electric Neon Green (`#00FF87` / `#10E57A`):** The primary optical trigger. Signifies real-time active states, primary kinetic actions, playback signals, affirmative vectors, and high-frequency analytical telemetry.
- **Secondary Accent — Vibrant Glowing Purple / Violet (`#9D4EDD` / `#7B2CBF`):** Represents chromatic depth, editorial complexity, secondary tags, and tension parameters.
- **Tertiary Flare — Neon Magenta (`#B5179E`):** Used for climax metrics, critical warnings, visceral narrative stakes, and high-energy analytical nodes.

### Canvas & Surface Structure
- **Pitch Obsidian Canvas (`#08090C`):** Base canvas substrate absorbing all diffuse reflections to ensure maximum dynamic contrast.
- **Charcoal Layer 1 (`#101217`):** Foundational container tier for panels, glass cards, and data matrices.
- **Charcoal Layer 2 (`#181A22`):** Elevated overlays, popovers, navigation rails, and modal sheets.
- **Foreground Elements:** High-contrast text `#F5F7FA` delivers crisp clarity; secondary metadata `#8A90A2` preserves scanning hierarchy without competing with active neon illumination.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Space Grotesk** across displays, titles, and body interfaces to enforce an auteur, high-concept cinematic tone. Its structural geometry, idiosyncratic curves, and modern proportions resonate with sci-fi projection titling and technical editing workstations.

Empirical readouts, timecodes, spectral frequencies, frame rates, and telemetry tags use **JetBrains Mono**. All metrics, labels, and status indicators formatted in `data-label` and `data-micro` must be set in full uppercase with tracking widened between `0.12em` and `0.16em` to ensure instantaneous optical legibility across dark luminous surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop viewports (1024px+), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet viewports (640px to 1023px), and a 4-column stack on mobile devices (<640px).

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Cinematic Framing:** Section breaks and hero backdrops rely on open `space-xl` intervals to isolate visual frames, akin to negative theater space around a silver screen.
- **Cockpit Density:** Controls, sensor chips, scrubber heads, and audio/visual metric meters utilize compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments to facilitate rapid cross-referencing without ocular strain.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through semi-transparent smoked glass planes, dark charcoal tonal tiers, and laser-precise chromatic glows rather than standard diffuse drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Darkroom Void - `#08090C`):** Base canvas. Absorbs ambient light and grounds optical content.
- **Level 1 (Smoked Glass Panel - `#101217` at 80% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`):** Standard containers and metric consoles. Outlined by a 1px ghost edge of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Glow Substrate - `#181A22` at 90% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`):** Focused cards, popovers, and scrubber surfaces. Border transitions to `rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.35)` or `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.40)`. Emits a localized optical bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.25), 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Level 3 (Neon Modal / Inspector Overlay):** Pitch charcoal frame with a double border effect: 1px outer stroke `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.5)` and a subtle interior green rim highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a sharp, controlled aesthetic calibrated at `roundedness: 1` (Soft: base radius `0.25rem` / 4px, `rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px, `rounded-xl: 0.75rem` / 12px).

Rounded pill shapes and inflated corner curves are deliberately avoided to maintain the architectural edge of studio mastering displays and cinematic viewfinders. Modals, cards, and input modules leverage 8px corners, while chips, interactive buttons, and telemetry flags stay at 4px. Full circular geometry is reserved strictly for playback scrubber beads, recording beads, and optical status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Neon Button:** Background `#00FF87`, text `#08090C`, font `Space Grotesk` Medium (uppercase, `0.06em` tracking). On hover, color transitions to `#10E57A` with an electric optical aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Neon Violet Button:** Background `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.15)`, text `#F5F7FA`, border `1px solid #9D4EDD`. On hover, fills with `rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.3)` and triggers a purple bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 18px rgba(157, 78, 221, 0.35)`).
- **Ghost Button:** Background `transparent`, text `#8A90A2`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On hover, border illuminates to `#00FF87` and text sharpens to `#F5F7FA`.

### Cards & Media Panels
- **Cinematic Neon Frame:** Smoked glass background (`#101217` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), 8px border radius, 1px perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Edge Glow:** On hover or selection, the card's top edge lights up via a horizontal gradient stroke (`transparent`, `#00FF87`, `#9D4EDD`, `transparent`), evoking laser-cut illumination.

### Chips & Parameter Tags
- **Inactive Tag:** 4px radius, filled with `#101217`, text `#8A90A2`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Neon Filter Chip:** Background `rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.1)`, text `#00FF87`, border `1px solid #00FF87`, accompanied by a 4px glowing neon indicator dot.

### Form Inputs & Search Terminals
- **Input Field:** Background `#101217`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F5F7FA`, placeholder `#8A90A2`.
- **Focus State:** Border sharpens to `#00FF87` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.25)`). Shortcut tags (e.g., `CTRL + F`) anchor flush right in `JetBrains Mono` at `#9D4EDD`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 16px square with 3px border radius. Unselected: `#101217` background with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` border. Selected: Solid `#00FF87` fill with `#08090C` check glyph and ambient glow.
- **Radios:** 16px circular base. Selected state displays an inner concentric `#9D4EDD` core surrounded by an electric violet halo.

### Spectrum Sliders & Scrubber Rails
- **Track:** 4px height in `#181A22`. Filled progress utilizes a dual gradient from `#00FF87` through `#9D4EDD`.
- **Scrubber Reticle:** 10px × 18px rectangular calibrated reticle with a central laser line indicator and neon green drop glow.
